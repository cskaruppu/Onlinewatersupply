# Coimbatore launch plan

## Why Coimbatore
- No dedicated tanker-booking app found for the city as of Sep 2026; most orders go by phone to local suppliers or through listing sites.
- Fast-growing apartment and IT belts (Saravanampatti, Kalapatti, Peelamedu, Vadavalli) rely on tankers when corporation supply (Siruvani / Pilloor) is irregular or in summer.

## Phase 1: pilot zone (weeks 1–8)
- Start in one belt: Saravanampatti – Kalapatti – Peelamedu – Ganapathy (north-east, Sathy Road / Avinashi Road).
- Sign up 20–30 tanker owners before launch; verify RC book, driving licence, and water source (licensed borewell / RO filling point).
- Target customers: apartment associations, hostels, hotels, construction sites, then individual homes.
- Fixed price card by capacity (3, 6, 9, 12, 24 KL) and distance band (A up to 5 km, B 5–10 km, C 10–15 km from the nearest filling point). No per-km charges. Publish it on the site. See the pricing section of `docs/platform-features.md`.

## Phase 2: city-wide (months 3–6)
- Add RS Puram, Gandhipuram, Singanallur, Kuniyamuthur, Thudiyalur, Podanur.
- Subscriptions for apartments (fixed weekly deliveries).
- Tamil + English interface; WhatsApp ordering for customers who don't install apps.

## Product (MVP)
1. Customer web app: phone OTP login, address + map pin, choose tanker, UPI (Razorpay/Cashfree) or cash, tracking, delivery OTP.
2. Owner/driver app: accept order, navigation, OTP check at delivery, weekly payout view.
3. Admin panel: owner verification, pricing per zone, disputes, refunds.

## Checks before launch
- Confirm any licensing needed from Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation for water transport/sale.
- Water quality: request test reports (TDS etc.) from filling points and show them to customers.
